# My AI Usage
I am at constant battle with myself on my stance for AI. I used to use it for everything from creating images to basic questions, but I have since been leaning towards less AI usage thanks to a wonderful video about D&D - [Should you use AI in your D&D games? - XP to Level 3 | Youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trq5vU7_bXk). I now use it to speed up code generation if I want to get it done quickly (e.g. Starting a project or stuck on a problem) and for questions Google can't answer, whether too complicated or unable to find.
I believe everyone has their own opinion on this topic, so whether you use AI or not, I am not fussed.
